About Us

Our story

Founded on love, respect and dignity

We Care Old Age was founded in 2015 with one goal: to treat every senior with love, respect and dignity.

Today we provide compassionate 24-hour residential care for seniors aged 60+, in a facility in Sandton that is safe, clean and designed to feel like a second home.

What guides us

Our mission and values

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality elderly care that promotes independence, health and happiness.

Our Values

  • Respect – treating all residents with dignity
  • Care – 24/7 professional and loving support
  • Community – creating a family environment

Our Promise

Every resident will be known by name, listened to, and cared for as we would care for our own family.

Respect

Every resident is treated with dignity in everything we do.

Care

Round-the-clock professional and loving support.

Community

A warm family environment where friendships grow.
